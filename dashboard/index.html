Interpretation

sky_scene is best separated: Cluster 2 achieves 66% dominant-fraction for sky_scene — the highest semantic purity of any cluster. This confirms CLIP's visual encoder strongly encodes sky-dominant scenes.

road_scene spreads across 5 clusters (0,1,3,4,5): Rather than a failure, this reveals that CLIP sub-divides road scenes by visual sub-type — likely highway vs. dense urban street vs. intersection. The clusters are semantically meaningful but finer-grained than the 4 coarse scene groups.

built_env captured by cluster 7: 49.1% dominant-fraction, intra-similarity 0.804 — the most cohesive non-sky cluster. Building-dominant images group naturally.

ARI=0.047 is expected: ARI penalises all inter-cluster splits of a single true class. With road_scene spanning 5 clusters, ARI is low by design. Purity (0.468) and NMI (0.101) are more informative metrics here.

Research Report

Research Question

"Can pretrained vision foundation models learn meaningful geographic representations of urban street scenes without task-specific supervision?"

Answer

Partially yes. Generic CLIP ViT-B/32, with zero fine-tuning, produces non-random urban scene structure in embedding space:

✅ sky_scene correctly isolated — clusters 2 & 6 are sky-dominant (66% / 37% purity)
✅ built_env captured — cluster 7 achieves 49% built_env purity
✅ Genuine visual spread — cosine range 0.375–0.949, not collapsed
✅ High-similarity communities — 3,147 edges at >0.85 threshold
⚠️ road_scene over-splits into 5 clusters (sub-type discrimination)
⚠️ Low overall scores (Sil=0.056, ARI=0.047) — expected without fine-tuning
